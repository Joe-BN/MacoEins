design desisions:
  - Open top:
    a top plate would ideally hold the switches and cover the components.
    however, the plate is part of the beauty, infact you could have it with a clear bottom to see the inside, also this version of the design does not have 'fragile' components per say.

    this, paired with the ease to trouble shoot and the 'minimal industrial' design made me go for the open top.

    the orientation allows you to use it however you wish to. pick a side and use it.

  - Open botom:
    included a design that leaves the bottom open to still view the internals.
    it alsto has an edge to mount a clear film or acrylic sheet if you want to (protect the inside and prevent snagging onto fabric while in your bag)
  
  - Battery situation:
    this design, as mentioned before, assumes you have already managed power and will use JST connector as an alternative to USB power.
    my assumption in this one is the use of sth like the 'pouch-style' batteries you get in old phones and dumbphones and wedge that in the space under the board.
    
    it, however does not yet, account for any other cell form factor withing the enclosure; which is weird for a bluetooth macropad. I'll change this in the next iteration. cylindrical 18650 cells or even double AAs might do. 
    Idk 

  - Tilt:
    this version doesn't have a tilt to it, might add that later but the plan was to sit as low as possible while still using cherry MX switches
